THE LEGEND OF POPRUS

A dark force has scattered the ancient Shadow Stones across the land. POPRUS begins a dangerous journey through the Wild Forest, Lost Village and Mountain Fortress. A loyal companion follows the hero into the unknown.

HOW TO PLAY

Use the arrow controls to move. Collect coins and energy crystals. Touch ATTACK near an enemy to defeat it. Reach the fortress to complete the mission.
